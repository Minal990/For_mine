✦ SOMETHING I WANTED TO WRITE ✦

From My Heart... 🤍

Not everything needs to be said out loud... some things are easier to write..... 🖋️

✦ ◇ ✦
💌

Pata nahi ye letter exactly kaise shuru karun... ..🌙

Shayad isliye....... kyunki aapke baare mein likhte waqt mujhe hamesha lagta hai ki jo kehna hai...... woh normal.... words mein properly aa hi nahi pata. Kuch........ cheezein bas andar hoti hain..... aur unhe explain karne ki koshish karo toh woh aur complicated lagne lagti hain. :)

Aapse meri...... koi lambi-lambi...... conversations nahi hui hain....... Humne saath mein...... koi hundred memories nahi banayi hain...... Phir bhi........... pata nahi kaise...... aap meri ........life ke un logon mein se ban gaye...... jinke.... baare mein main bina kisi reason ke soch leti hoon.......🫠

Kabhi aapki....... koi photo saamne aa jaaye.... kabhi koi video mil jaaye.... kabhi bas aapka naam kahin dikh jaaye... aur pata nahi kyun.... mere face pe... automatically ek smile aa jaati hai... 😭

Aur haan... mujhe khud bhi nahi..... pata ki ye habit kab itni serious ho gayi...... ki aapki photos........ aur videos save karna... ek normal si......... baat lagne lagi.....😂
I guess I've officially developed a habit of saving your videos... 👀🎥

Aapki ek cheez ....mujhe genuinely .......bahut achhi lagti hai — aapko dekhkar........ kabhi aisa nahi lagta........ ki aapko kisi aur jaisa banne ki zarurat hai.....

Shayad.... isi wajah se aap mujhe......... itne naturally achhe lagte hain....✨

Main aapko idealize karne ki koshish nahi karti....... Mujhe aapki har...... cheez perfect lagti ho...... aisa bhi nahi hai.... Bas jo aap ho..... us version..... ko dekhkar mere andar ek ajeeb .....si softness aa jaati hai.....

Aur shayad isi liye...... aapke liye likhna mujhe achha lagta hai....🪶

Kabhi kabhi main sochti hoon...... agar aapko mere dimaag ki woh saari random thoughts dikh... jaayein jo aapse .....related hoti hain........ toh shayad aap pehle hasoge........ phir mujhe pagal samjhoge....😂😭

Lekin sach kahun.....?

Mujhe aapse kuch prove nahi karna.....

Na mujhe kisi cheez ko naam dena hai..... na har.... feeling ka answer chahiye...... na mujhe ye jaana hai... ki aap mere..... baare mein exactly kya sochte hain....

Bas..... jo meri taraf se hai.... woh real hai....🩶

Aur shayad...... mere liye itna hi kaafi hai.....

Aap meri story.... ka koi dramatic character banke nahi aaye the..... Bas pata nahi kab..... ek normal sa naam.... meri favourite thoughts ka part ban gaya....

Aur.... birthday par aapko ye letter..... dene ka reason bhi bas itna hai.....

Aaj aapka din hai... toh mujhe.... laga ki ek baar woh sab likh doon.... jo shayad saamne bolne par...... main khud hi awkward hokar chup jaaun.....😭

◇ ✦ ◇

So... Happy Birthday, you.....🎂

I hope jab aap ye padhoge..... aapke .....face par ek chhoti si smile aaye.....

Aur agar aayi.....
toh samajh lena.... ye letter likhne ka mera reason wahi tha..... :) 🫶🏻
